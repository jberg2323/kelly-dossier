To the moon

The moves that turn a good friend into a best friend for life.

Be identical in every room

Neptune on her Ascendant means she reads incongruence instantly. The friend who's the same at the burger joint, the fundraiser and the hard phone call is the one she keeps.

Remember everything

Use the notes box. A 4/6 chooses her inner circle by who pays attention. Quoting her back a month later is worth a hundred compliments.

Give her your people

Fourth-line profiles grow through network. Introduce her to the best humans you know with a specific reason each time. You're built for this.

Celebrate loud, mourn quiet

Her wins get the group text. Her losses get a text that says "I'm around, no reply needed" and then actual silence.

Never make her the caretaker

Soul Urge 33 people get used. Be the friend who needs nothing from her. That's the rarest thing in her life.

Name the ritual

Burger night needs a name, a scorecard and a trophy. Taurus loves tradition; Gemini loves the bit.

Own May 1

Plan her birthday like a product launch. Taurus birthdays are a loyalty test and almost everyone fails it.

Be the 1 AM call

Say it once, plainly: "If it goes sideways, you call me, any hour." Then answer when it does. That's the whole moon.
